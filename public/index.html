Game designer and developer from Malaysia here! I live with a dream to make games that people can love, remember, and be inspired by for years to come.
I first started game development as a hobby at 13, and grew over the years into a serious pursuit of a career in the game industry. Currently, my focus is on building my portfolio and gain a lot of new skills.
My primary game engine is Godot (both GDScript and C#), but I also sometimes work with Unity (C#). I have transferable skills in game programming and design, and I'm always looking to expand my technical and creative abilities.
I also firmly believe that passion, ambition, and determination to be the best version of yourself, can bring the best games into the world! At the same time, I'm a lifelong learner who's eager to learn about new areas of game development.
Here, you can find my journey, projects, and things I've learned along the way — from game jams to fun personal projects I've done.
